# SUTRA — UI/UX research source register (2026-10-08)

Companion to `SUTRA_UIUX_FRONTEND_RESEARCH_2026-10-08.md`. **This register governs interface research
only.** Data-policy, geocoding, evaluation and system sources remain in `PS3_SOURCE_REGISTER.md`
(`[S1]`–`[S96]`) and that register is unchanged by this pass. Nothing here may be used to introduce an
external dataset, an external geography or a new precision mechanism `[S95]`.

`[G]` practitioner guidance · `[D]` design-system reference. **Use**: what SUTRA takes from it.
